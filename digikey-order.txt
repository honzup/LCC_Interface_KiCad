Packaging reminder (Digi-Key suffix):
  -1-ND = Cut Tape  (what you want for small qty)
  -2-ND = Tape & Reel (full reel - avoid)
  -6-ND = Digi-Reel  (custom qty on mini-reel, +fee)
  Through-hole parts (RJ45, header) have no Cut Tape - "Bulk"/"Tube" is correct.

Caps are generic: any 100nF and any 4.7uF (16V+) X7R 0805 will do - brand/exact
part number does not matter, only value / size (0805) / dielectric (X7R) / voltage.
